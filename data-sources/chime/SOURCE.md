# Prayer-alert chime

- **File**: `android/app/src/main/res/raw/takrar_chime.wav` (Android raw resource `takrar_chime`,
  played by the `prayer-chime-v1` notification channel)
- **Origin**: our own work. Generated by `scripts/build-chime.mjs` from sine waves: two soft bell
  tones (E5 then C5, each a fundamental with two quieter overtones), a 12 ms raised-cosine attack,
  an exponential ring-out and a 200 ms fade to silence. No recording, sample or third-party sound
  is used.
- **Format**: PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, 2.0 s, 176,444 bytes
- **sha256**: `50e80b1511a6c41ddb2db4549704ac7873620f967b397cad5589e2e67081abd5`
- **Licence**: Takrar's own work; no third-party rights. Listed in `data-sources/licenses.d/25-chime.txt`
  so the licences page says where the sound comes from.
- **Created**: 2026-11-13
- **Regenerate / check**: `node scripts/build-chime.mjs` / `node scripts/build-chime.mjs --check`
  (the check allows one step of rounding difference per sample between JavaScript engines)
