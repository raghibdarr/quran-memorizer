=== Mosque lists: OpenStreetMap
Data © OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The mosque lists in the app (data/mosques/<country>.json) are a Derivative Database made from
OpenStreetMap features tagged amenity=place_of_worship and religion=muslim: each mosque's map
position, rounded, and its name. They are offered under the same licence. The files, and the
script that builds them (scripts/build-mosques.mjs), are public in the app's source repository,
so anyone can get, rebuild and reuse them. The app loads one country's list when you ask for it,
sorts it by distance on your device, and never sends your position anywhere.

Country assignment at build time used Natural Earth 1:10m admin-0 countries (public domain,
https://www.naturalearthdata.com); the boundaries themselves are not included in the app.
