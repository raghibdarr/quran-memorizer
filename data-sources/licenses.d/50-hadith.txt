=== Hadith: the Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi
al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyya by Yahya ibn Sharaf al-Nawawi (d. 676 AH). Classical works in the public
domain; transcribed, vocalised and translated by Takrar; collated against the witnesses listed per
item (Ibn Daqiq al-'Id's Sharh al-Arba'in and Ibn Hajar al-Haytami's al-Fath al-Mubin, read on
shamela.ws, with hadith-api's ara-nawawi edition for comparison only). The English translation is
Takrar's own work. Nothing from Sunnah.com's English, Darussalam or any published translation of
the Forty is used.
