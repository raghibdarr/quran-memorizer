=== City names and positions: GeoNames
Place names and positions: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), from the cities15000 extract
retrieved 2026-11-13 (https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip). Licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data is provided "as is" without warranty
or any representation of accuracy, timeliness or completeness.

Changes: trimmed to name, country, latitude and longitude (rounded to 2 decimal places) and time
zone, and re-shaped into public/data/cities.json by scripts/build-cities.mjs. Used for city
search, the place label after a location fix, and each place's time zone, all on your device.
