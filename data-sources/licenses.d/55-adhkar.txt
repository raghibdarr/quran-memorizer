=== Adhkar and duas
The adhkar and duas decks (morning and evening, after the prayer, sleeping and waking, duas for
the day, the words of the prayer) quote hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi
Dawud, Jami' al-Tirmidhi and Sunan Ibn Majah: classical works in the public domain. The Arabic
is transcribed and vowelled by Takrar from the printed editions as al-Maktaba al-Shamila
(shamela.ws) gives them, and collated against fawazahmed0/hadith-api for comparison only.
Gradings quote al-Tirmidhi's Jami', al-Nawawi's al-Adhkar (read in the al-Arna'ut edition on
shamela.ws) and al-Daraqutni's Sunan; each is attributed by name on the item. The English
translations are Takrar's own work. Nothing from Hisn al-Muslim, Sunnah.com's English,
Darussalam or any published translation is used. Quranic items are references only and render
from the app's licensed Quran text.
