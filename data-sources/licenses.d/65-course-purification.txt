=== Foundations course, Unit Purification
The lessons, recall cards, check questions and glossary definitions of the purification
unit are Takrar's own work, written for this app.

Each school's position is cited, by work and chapter, to one classical primer, with a short
phrase of its Arabic quoted as the words relied on:
  Hanafi:   Mukhtasar al-Quduri, by Abu al-Husayn al-Quduri (d. 428 AH)
  Maliki:   al-Risala, by Ibn Abi Zayd al-Qayrawani (d. 386 AH)
  Shafi'i:  Matn Abi Shuja' (al-Ghaya wa al-Taqrib), by Abu Shuja' al-Asfahani (d. 593 AH)
  Hanbali:  'Umdat al-Fiqh, by Ibn Qudamah al-Maqdisi (d. 620 AH)
Points of agreement and difference are cited to Bidayat al-Mujtahid by Ibn Rushd (d. 595 AH),
al-Ijma' by Ibn al-Mundhir (d. 318 AH) and Maratib al-Ijma' by Ibn Hazm (d. 456 AH).

These works are in the public domain. They are cited and briefly quoted, not reproduced; no
edition's notes, introduction or apparatus is used. The quotations were read in the copies at
shamela.ws (al-Maktaba al-Shamila), page by page, as recorded in
content/deen/course/purification/citations.json. Quran references are rendered from the
app's own Quran text.
