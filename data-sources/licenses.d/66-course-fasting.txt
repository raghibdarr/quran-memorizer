=== Course, Unit Fasting
The lessons, cards, questions, glossary entries and English wording of Unit Fasting are
Takrar's own work.

School positions are cited, by work and chapter with a short Arabic phrase, from four
classical primers, all long in the public domain: Mukhtasar al-Quduri (al-Quduri, d. 428 AH),
al-Risala (Ibn Abi Zayd al-Qayrawani, d. 386 AH), Matn Abi Shuja' (Abu Shuja' al-Asfahani,
d. 593 AH) and 'Umdat al-Fiqh (Ibn Qudamah, d. 620 AH). Agreement and difference are cited
from Bidayat al-Mujtahid (Ibn Rushd, d. 595 AH) and al-Ijma' (Ibn al-Mundhir, d. 318 AH),
also public domain. These works are cited, not reproduced.

Three hadith are quoted in Arabic from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sunan Abi Dawud (public-domain
texts, our own transcription collated against two witnesses) with our own English translation.
Quran passages are shown from the app's licensed Quran text and translation, listed above.

Online copies were read to locate and check the wording (shamela.ws, dorar.net, hadith-api).
No text, translation or commentary from those sites is bundled.
