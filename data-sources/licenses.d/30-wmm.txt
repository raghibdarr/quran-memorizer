=== Qibla compass: World Magnetic Model 2025 (NOAA NCEI and British Geological Survey)
The qibla compass corrects the phone's magnetic heading to true north with the
World Magnetic Model WMM2025 (released 13 November 2024), produced by the US National Oceanic and
Atmospheric Administration's National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI) and the British
Geological Survey. Takrar evaluates NOAA's published coefficient file on your device with its own
code; nothing is sent anywhere. The model is a work of the US government and is in the public
domain. https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model

The qibla bearing comes from adhan (above). The sun's position for the sun aid is worked out on
your device with Takrar's own implementation of Jean Meeus's published formulas (Astronomical
Algorithms, 2nd edition, 1998).
