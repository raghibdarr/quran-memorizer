An old method, kept

A day of hifz

Takrar plans the traditional day for you and keeps the count. These times are an example; you choose how much time you have, and the plan fits around it.

Miss a day and nothing falls apart: a streak freeze covers it, and the reviews wait for you in order of need.

Morning: the new lesson

  • Listen, with each word's meaning5 min
  • Memorise, six, four, four and six times15 min
  • Recite the whole lesson from memory3 min

What's inside

Reviews that come back on time

Every ayah you learn gets its own schedule. Recite it cleanly and it returns less often; stumble and it comes back tomorrow. The ayahs you keep slipping on are gathered into a short drill.

The mushaf, page for page

Read the Madina 1441H print or the 15-line IndoPak, with every ayah on the page you know it from. Hide the words and reveal them as you recite, to test yourself a whole page at a time.

Listen the whole way through

Play a surah, a page or a whole juz with the screen off. Slow it down, loop a passage, pause between ayahs to repeat, or download a surah for when you have no signal.

Recite, and it listens

Turn on the voice check and recite from memory. It follows you word by word and marks a skipped or changed word, without your recitation ever leaving the device.

The board, kept

In Quran schools across Africa, a student writes the day’s lesson on a wooden board. In Takrar, the lesson is a few ayahs, heard with the meaning of every word.

Questions

Is it really free? Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and nothing locked behind a payment.

Do I need an account? No. Everything works and is saved on your device. Sign in only if you want your progress on more than one device.

What does 6, 4, 4, 6 mean? Each new ayah is recited twenty times in four rounds: six with the text, four from memory, four with the text again and six from memory. Then you put its words back in order.

What are sabaq, sabqi and manzil? The three parts of the traditional hifz day: the new lesson, the recent lessons, and everything older. Takrar schedules all three and puts your weakest ayahs first.

Does the voice check upload my recitation? No. The recognition model runs on your phone or in your browser, and your voice stays there.

Which mushaf does it use? The Madina 1441H print, page for page, and the 15-line IndoPak layout. You can switch between them and keep your place.

Does it work offline? Lessons and reviews keep working once they have loaded. Download a surah’s recitation to listen without a connection.

Is it only in English? For now the app, the meanings and the transliteration are in English. More languages are planned.

Can my family read together? Yes. A khatm circle lets a family or a class share out the thirty juz and mark each one read.

What do you keep about me? As little as possible. The privacy page lists exactly what is stored and where, and you can delete your account from the app.
