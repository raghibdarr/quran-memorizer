Word timings: quran-align
==============================================================================

Word-precise timings for EveryAyah recordings by Colin Fair, https://github.com/cpfair/quran-align
(release 2016-11-24), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Renumbered to Takrar's word list.

==============================================================================

Timestamps by Qur'anic Universal Audio, https://github.com/QUD-Technologies/quranic-universal-audio,

the property of their reciters and are streamed from MP3Quran.net and QuranicAudio.com.


==============================================================================
Recitations
==============================================================================

Per-ayah recordings stream from EveryAyah.com; surah recordings from MP3Quran.net and
QuranicAudio.com. Takrar doesn't host them; a surah you download is saved on your device only.

  From quran-madina-html 2.0.0 by Tarek Eldeeb (https://github.com/tarekeldeeb/quran-madina-html),
