Assalamu alaikum,

I'm building Takrar (https://takrar.pro), a free app for memorising the Quran. It has no ads and
no payments, and works on the web, Android and iPhone. Lessons go one ayah at a time, and learners
can tap a word to see its meaning, which helps them understand what they are memorising.

We'd like to ship the Quranic Arabic Corpus word-by-word English gloss inside the app, so lessons
work offline. Before doing that, I want to make sure we have your permission, because the Corpus
terms mention the GPL, verbatim copies, and a request not to use the data commercially.

What we would do:
- Bundle the gloss unchanged, keyed by chapter:verse:word, with the Corpus copyright notice in the
  data file.
- Credit it in the app as "Word-by-word English: Quranic Arabic Corpus (corpus.quran.com)", with
  a link, wherever we list our sources.
- Keep it free: no ads, no paywall around it. If we ever accept donations, they would be optional
  and would never unlock content.

Could you confirm whether that is acceptable? If you would prefer a different attribution, a
different source file, or a particular version, please let me know. If it isn't something you
can permit, I'd be grateful to know that too, so we can write our own glosses instead.
