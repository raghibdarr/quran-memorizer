# Errata

Corrections to shipped deen content, newest first. Each entry: date, item id and rev, what
was wrong, what changed, who found it. The `/sources` page shows this list. An entry is one
line holding an ISO date and the item as `<deck>:<itemId>`, for example
`- 2026-12-01 · nawawi40:02 rev 2: a vowel on the third word fixed (found by …)`.

- 2026-11-15 · nawawi40:07 rev 2: the note said, after the printed commentary, that Tamim al-Dari narrated no hadith but this one. Al-Nawawi's own wording limits that to Sahih Muslim, and other collections relate hadith from Tamim, so the sentence was dropped from the note. The Arabic and the English of the hadith are unchanged (found by the content audit).
- 2026-11-15 · duas-daily:iftar rev 2: al-Daraqutni's verdict "its chain is hasan" was cited as read on a website. It was located again in the Mu'assasat al-Risala print (vol. 3 p. 156, after no. 2279) and the reference now names that edition. The Arabic and the English are unchanged (found by the content audit).
- 2026-11-15 · hijri-events:six-shawwal: the description named al-Shafi'i and Ahmad as recommending the six fasts and Malik and Abu Hanifa as disliking them, with no source. Only Malik's dislike was located (Ibn Rushd, Bidayat al-Mujtahid, the recommended fasts), so the line now says the schools differ and names that alone (found by the content audit).
- 2026-11-15 · zakat-notes:debts and zakat-notes:hawl: two Arabic quotes from Bidayat al-Mujtahid each carried a copying slip (الزكاة الناض for زكاة الناض; يستقل به الحول for يستقبل به الحول). Both now follow the Dar al-Hadith print. The English notes are unchanged (found by the content audit).
