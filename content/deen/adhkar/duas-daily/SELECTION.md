## Not included, and why

- **Entering the home, "Allahumma inni as'aluka khayra al-mawlij…"** (Abu Dawud 5096): its
  chain is broken, and no verdict of sahih or hasan was located. Muslim 2018 speaks of
  remembering Allah on entering, without giving words.
- **"Bismillah" before eating as a separate item**: the sound hadith (al-Bukhari 5376) tells the
  boy to mention Allah's name, an instruction rather than a wording; the forgot-Bismillah item
  gives the words.
- **"Allahumma laka sumtu…" as the main iftar dua**: mursal (Mu'adh ibn Zuhrah does not name who
  told him); shown only as a variant under the default, with al-Nawawi's remark.
