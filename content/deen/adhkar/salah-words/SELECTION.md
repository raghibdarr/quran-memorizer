## Not included, and why

- **"Rabbi ighfir li, rabbi ighfir li" between the prostrations** (Abu Dawud 874, Ibn Majah 897,
  from Hudhayfah): no verdict of sahih or hasan was located that we could quote (al-Nawawi gives
  the narration in al-Adhkar no. 155 without one). Its Essentials card was removed in the audit.
- **"Subhanaka Allahumma…" as the Prophet's own words** (Abu Dawud 775-776, al-Tirmidhi
  242-243): those compilers themselves criticise the chains, so the item cites Umar's practice in
  Muslim 399 instead, and says so.
- **The count of three in bowing and prostration** (Abu Dawud 886, al-Tirmidhi 261): al-Tirmidhi
  says its chain is not connected. The items give no count, and the Essentials cards lost theirs.
