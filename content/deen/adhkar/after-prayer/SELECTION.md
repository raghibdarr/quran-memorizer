## Not included, and why

- **Subhan Allah, al-hamdu lillah, Allahu akbar, thirty-three times each** as one recited item:
  the hadith (Muslim 595, 596, 597) give the count, not one fixed wording, and the narrators of
  Muslim 595 explain the order differently. The tasbih counter covers it, and Pray it now links
  to it before the words that complete the hundred.
- **The Mu'awwidhat after every prayer** (Abu Dawud 1523; al-Tirmidhi 2903): al-Tirmidhi calls
  it gharib only, and no verdict of sahih or hasan was located that we could quote.
- **Ayat al-Kursi after every prayer**: in al-Nasa'i's al-Sunan al-Kubra, which we could not
  collate against two witnesses.
