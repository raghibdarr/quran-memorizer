## Not included, and why

- **Subhan Allah 33, al-hamdu lillah 33, Allahu akbar 34 at bedtime** (al-Bukhari 6318, the
  hadith of Ali and Fatimah): the hadith gives the counts as an instruction, not one wording to
  recite. Use the tasbih counter.
- **"Al-hamdu lillah alladhi 'afani fi jasadi…" on waking** (al-Tirmidhi 3401): not collated
  against two witnesses in this pass.
