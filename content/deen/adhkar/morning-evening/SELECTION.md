# Morning and evening: selection

Our own selection (stage A), made from the hadith and the Quran directly. It does not follow
the order, contents or English of Hisn al-Muslim or any other compilation.

Rule for all five adhkar decks: an item ships only if its words are in al-Bukhari or Muslim, or
at least one named authority's verdict of sahih or hasan was located and quoted (spec 5.1), and
its Arabic and citation numbers were confirmed in two witnesses (`tools/seal.mjs`,
`tools/witness.mjs verify --all`). Narrations with disputed chains are left out.

## Not included, and why

- **Ayat al-Kursi in the morning and evening**: the narration that names it for morning and
  evening (Ubayy ibn Ka'b) is in al-Nasa'i's al-Sunan al-Kubra and al-Hakim, which we could not
  collate against two witnesses. Ayat al-Kursi is in the sleep deck, from al-Bukhari 2311. The
  Essentials card that put it under morning and evening was removed in the audit.
- **"Raditu billahi rabban…" three times**: the chains are disputed (Abu Dawud 5072 through an
  unnamed servant; al-Tirmidhi calls his narration, 3389, hasan gharib, and others weakened it).
- **"Allahumma inni asbahtu ushhiduka…"** (Abu Dawud 5069), **"Allahumma ma asbaha bi min
  ni'matin…"** (Abu Dawud 5073), **"Hasbiya Allahu…" seven times** (Abu Dawud 5081): chains
  questioned; no verdict of sahih or hasan located that we could quote from its work.
- **"Ya Hayyu ya Qayyum bi-rahmatika astaghith…"** and **"Asbahna 'ala fitrat al-Islam…"**: in
  al-Nasa'i's Kubra, al-Hakim and Ahmad, which we could not collate against two witnesses.
